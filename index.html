Nikita Sarawgi

I am a robotics and AI engineer at GrayMatter Robotics, working on decision-making for robots in semi-structured environments for high-mix manufacturing.

I completed my M.S. in Computer Science at USC in December 2025, advised by Satyandra K. Gupta and Daniel Seita, and worked closely with Omey M. Manyar.

I want to build robots that are robust and reliable enough to be deployed in production and to work alongside people. I believe this requires structured, compositional systems built on learned abstractions, so that a robot’s decisions are interpretable and its components can be reused across tasks and environments. My interests center on four connected questions: what state and action abstractions a robot should learn, how skills and behaviors should be bounded and composed for long-horizon tasks, how a robot can continually detect and acquire knowledge it is missing, and how that knowledge can be integrated into its existing abstractions for later reuse.

I am currently applying for PhD positions for Fall 2027.

Preference-Conditioned Reinforcement Learning for Space-Time Efficient Online 3D Bin Packing
Nikita Sarawgi, Omey M. Manyar, Fan Wang, Thinh H. Nguyen, Daniel Seita, Satyandra K. Gupta
ICRA, 2026
project page / arXiv / code

An RL policy for online 3D bin packing that chooses both the next item to pack and the face to grasp it on. A preference parameter set at inference time trades off packing density against operation time without retraining.

Autonomous Execution of Insertion Operations in Space Assembly Tasks
Abhay Negi, Nikita Sarawgi, Dhanush Penmetsa, Hantao Ye, Omey M. Manyar, Ashtin Cheng, Satyandra K. Gupta
AIAA SciTech Forum (ISAM IV), 2025
paper

An autonomous insertion system for in-space assembly. The robot first estimates a coarse pose to position a wrist-mounted camera near the target, then uses a learned model to estimate fine misalignment, and executes the insertion under impedance control.
